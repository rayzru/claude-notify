## Hidden while you share the screen

While the screen is mirrored, shared in a call or recorded, macOS hides every notification
— from every app, this one included. Nothing says so: they simply do not appear.

To see them anyway:

*System Settings → Notifications → Allow notifications → When mirroring or sharing the
display*

The step ticks itself once that is on. Leave it off if you would rather nobody watching your
screen saw them; they still wait in Notification Centre.
